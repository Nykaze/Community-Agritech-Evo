Backport
--------
The port to Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge was done by Nykaze, including:

  - Porting the mod to Minecraft 1.20.1 and Forge 47.x.
  - Fixing the pre-1.20.1 resource layout (recipe/, loot_table/, advancement/
    and assets/items/ were migrated to the 1.20.1 folder names).
  - Fixing duplicate recipe IDs caused by a hardcoded id in
    DurabilityShapelessRecipeSerializer.
  - Fixing a lossy ingredient encoder that dropped tag identity, which caused
    tilling recipes to require a barrier instead of a hoe.
  - Adding the crop trait system (growth, yield, resistance, mutability).
  - Fixing harvest output stacking in the basic and advanced planters.
  - Making the JEI and Jade integrations optional at runtime.
